# 0028 · Risks worth naming before starting

- **The Swete → Rahlfs table is the plan's real unknown.** Nobody has published one, and the size estimate in [03-versification.md](03-versification.md) is unmeasured. 0028.1 measures it before any schema exists. If more than about 1,000 verses differ, or whole books are ordered differently, stop and decide again whether storing Rahlfs numbers is worth it compared with keeping Swete numbers plus a direct TVTMS-style table.
- **"Rahlfs numbering" is not the Rahlfs text.** Swete follows codex B, while Rahlfs is an eclectic text, so a reader may find a word "missing" that their printed Rahlfs has. The panel says "nach Swete" in its heading, and the hover card never claims to quote Rahlfs.
- **AI parses teach confident nonsense at scale.** Roughly half a million words (unmeasured) will be proposed by a model. Only confirmed tags are public, proposals are visible only to maintainers, and "Kapitel bestätigen (sichere)" never confirms `low` words. The signal that review can't keep up is open proposals growing faster than confirmed ones over a month. Then narrow the runs to the books being read, and don't widen the public view.
- **Pass-1 proposals look more certain than they are.** A form with one NT analysis can have another in the LXX (Hebraisms, other senses). They stay proposals and go through the same review, and the review page shows `Source` so the maintainer knows where a tag came from.
- **Hebrew link anchors depend on STEP word ids.** `LxxHebrewLinks.StepHebrewWordId` breaks when a STEP re-import renumbers `StepHebrewWords`. The seed stores `SourceKey`s and re-resolves them (0028.8). A STEP update without a re-run of the LXX link import would leave dangling links, so the STEP import gate re-resolves them when it inserts Hebrew words.
- **GPL upstream.** eliranwong's repo is GPL-3.0, even though Swete is public domain. We only download the files and never commit them; the seed files hold references and hashes, not Greek text, which 0028.8 checks. If the upstream repo disappears, a fresh install has no LXX text. The fallback is our own transcription from the archive.org scans, which is out of scope.
- **Cost of the AI runs is unknown.** Packages carry the Hebrew verse and TBESG candidates, so they are larger than a mapping package. 0028.7 logs tokens per verse on Ruth, and that number decides whether the whole OT is run at once or book by book.
- **Assumption: TVTMS's Greek column follows Rahlfs closely enough.** TVTMS says "Greek by LXX" and has a separate Greek2 (Brenton) column. Spot checks (Jer 25:15 → 32:1, Ps 10:1 → 9:22) match Rahlfs. 0028.2's unit tests pin the five examples, and any later mismatch goes into `swete-to-rahlfs.csv` with a note.
