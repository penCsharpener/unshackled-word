- **0023.2 done (2026-11-22, backend only):** version 0.9.2, no migration. `Kind = verses` (start < end, same chapter, both verses in Elb1871) and `Kind = words` (one verse, `startPosition..endPosition` must all exist in `Elb1871Words`; `quotedText` is rebuilt server-side from `WordInContext` joined by spaces, max 500 characters, a client quote is ignored; the verse end is normalised to the start). `PUT api/notes/{id}` with an `anchor` re-anchors with the same validation and a refreshed quote. Read side: `GET api/notes/chapter/{bookId}/{chapter}?level=<0..1000>` (omitted or 0 = Elb1871 only; otherwise the same variant selection as the reader via `RevisionSql.EffectiveVerses`). Contract fields for the frontend: `anchorState` (`ok` | `drifted` | `noBaseWords`), `mappedWords[]` (`revisionWordId`, `positionInVerse`, `basePositionInVerse`; the variant words that carry the marker) and `revisionVerseId`, both only on words notes whose verse has a variant at the requested level; without them use `anchor.startPosition..endPosition` (Elb1871). `drifted` = the Elb1871 words at the positions differ from `anchor.quotedText` after normalising whitespace and punctuation (letters/digits only, case-sensitive) or a position is gone; then no mapping is returned and the note belongs at verse level with the quote as hint. `noBaseWords` = a variant exists but no variant word has a base position inside the range (`mappedWords` empty). `GET` responses of POST/PUT carry no level (Elb1871 state). Deviations: a variant-only word cannot be anchored by construction (positions are Elb1871 positions, anything outside the Elb1871 verse is refused with 400); a verse-level note on a verse without text still only checks the verse exists; the `level` validation error key is `level.Value`. Tests: `NoteValidatorsTests`, `NoteServiceTests` (quote normalisation, drift, mapping), `NoteDatabaseTests` (ok, drifted after changing a `WordInContext`, positions gone, variant mapping incl. `noBaseWords` on a test revision).
- **0023.2f done (2026-11-22):** version 0.9.3. Word selection in the reader (`lib/notes/selection.ts`): mouse drag or click + shift-click across word buttons (also Shift+Enter from the keyboard), touch long-press (500 ms) on the first word and tap on the last; the selected words get `bg-mark-note` (new token `--uw-mark-note`, light/dark). A floating action behind the last selected word offers "Notiz" (words note, Elb1871 positions, through `basePosition` on a variant), "Notiz zu V. x–y" when the selection crosses verses, "Notiz zu V. x" when a selected word exists only in the variant; Esc or a press outside clears it. Shift-click on a second verse's "Notiz zum Vers" turns the open verse draft into a range. Markers: words notes behind their last shown word (`mappedWords` at a revision level), verse-range notes at the first verse with a bracket line in the verse gutter from 64rem, drifted/`noBaseWords`/unresolvable notes at the verse number. The notes list is fetched with `?level=` of the reader. `NotesPanel` shows the anchor line („quote“ · Römer 8,28 or Römer 8,28–30), the drift hint and "Neu verankern" for own drifted notes (banner above the text, the next selection's action "Hier verankern" sends `PUT` with the new anchor and the unchanged body); Esc closes the notes sheet (not while typing or in a dialog). Deviations: `GET chapterWords` now also returns `basePositionInVerse` for variant words (backend: `ChapterWordsQuery` revised SQL + `WordResponse`), because the frontend had no other way to know a variant word's Elb1871 position; the verse number itself is not clickable, the range comes from shift-click on the verse note action or a cross-verse word selection; in re-anchor mode a plain word click selects the word instead of opening the word detail; the bracket shows from 64rem (`laptop`), not only on the 120rem layout; the floating action covers part of the next line while open; markers of several notes on the same last word are grouped with a count, like verse markers. Tests: `lib/notes/selection.test.ts`, `ChapterPage.selection.test.tsx` (drag → words payload, shift-click across verses → verses payload, verse-draft range, touch long-press, marker placement incl. bracket and drift, `mappedWords` at level 300, variant-only word, re-anchor PUT, Esc). Smoke-tested anonymously on `/bible/45/8` (drag over „Gott lieben,“, action, sign-in prompt, Esc). Open: walk-through with a signed-in user on a phone and on a revised level with real data.
