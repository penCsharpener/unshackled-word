- **0023.2 done (2026-11-22, backend only):** version 0.9.2, no migration. `Kind = verses` (start < end, same chapter, both verses in Elb1871) and `Kind = words` (one verse, `startPosition..endPosition` must all exist in `Elb1871Words`; `quotedText` is rebuilt server-side from `WordInContext` joined by spaces, max 500 characters, a client quote is ignored; the verse end is normalised to the start). `PUT api/notes/{id}` with an `anchor` re-anchors with the same validation and a refreshed quote. Contract fields for the frontend: `anchorState` (`ok` | `drifted` | `noBaseWords`), `mappedWords[]` (`revisionWordId`, `positionInVerse`, `basePositionInVerse`; the variant words that carry the marker) and `revisionVerseId`, both only on words notes whose verse has a variant at the requested level; without them use `anchor.startPosition..endPosition` (Elb1871). `drifted` = the Elb1871 words at the positions differ from `anchor.quotedText` after normalising whitespace and punctuation (letters/digits only, case-sensitive) or a position is gone; then no mapping is returned and the note belongs at verse level with the quote as hint. `noBaseWords` = a variant exists but no variant word has a base position inside the range (`mappedWords` empty). `GET` responses of POST/PUT carry no level (Elb1871 state). Deviations: a variant-only word cannot be anchored by construction (positions are Elb1871 positions, anything outside the Elb1871 verse is refused with 400); a verse-level note on a verse without text still only checks the verse exists; the `level` validation error key is `level.Value`. Tests: `NoteValidatorsTests`, `NoteServiceTests` (quote normalisation, drift, mapping), `NoteDatabaseTests` (ok, drifted after changing a `WordInContext`, positions gone, variant mapping incl. `noBaseWords` on a test revision).
